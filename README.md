# Facebook Shadowban: Signs and How to Fix It (2026 Guide)

If your Facebook reach suddenly dropped, you're not alone. Meta has been cracking down hard on spam, fake engagement and copied content. Facebook removed more than **20 million accounts impersonating major creators in 2025**, and now warns that reposting other people's videos, or making only small edits like adding captions or borders, can lead to **reduced distribution** and loss of recommendation or monetization eligibility, according to [BusinessDay's March 2026 report](https://businessday.ng/technology/article/facebook-removes-20m-fake-creator-accounts-as-it-boosts-original-content/).

The scale of enforcement is huge. Facebook took action on about **3.5 billion fake accounts in 2025**, according to [PPC Land's analysis of Meta's Transparency Center data](https://ppc.land/facebook-banned-3-5-billion-fake-accounts-in-2025-vab-analysis-finds/). With systems that big, even honest pages sometimes get caught and see their reach fall.

In this guide, I'll explain what Facebook shadow banning really is, the signs to look for, how to check your status, how to get unshadowbanned on Facebook and how to avoid it in the future.

## What Is a Facebook Shadow Ban?

**A Facebook shadow ban is when your posts, page or profile are quietly shown to fewer people, without Facebook sending you a clear warning or notice.**

Meta doesn't officially use the term "shadowban." Instead, it talks about **reduced distribution** and content that is **not eligible for recommendations**. In practice, the effect is the same:

- Your posts still appear to your followers, but fewer people see them
- Your content stops showing in Feed recommendations, Reels or search
- Your reach, likes and comments drop suddenly

Facebook's audience is massive, which is why reach changes matter so much. ## Why Does Facebook Shadow Banning Happen?

**Facebook usually limits reach when content breaks Community Standards, looks spammy, is unoriginal or comes from accounts with suspicious behavior.**

Common causes:

- **Unoriginal content:** Reposting other creators' videos or photos without adding value
- **Spammy behavior:** Posting too often, mass tagging or repeating the same comment
- **Engagement bait:** "Like if you agree," "Share to win" or "Comment YES"
- **Clickbait links** or low-quality websites
- **Fake engagement:** Buying likes, followers or using engagement groups
- **Community Standards violations:** Hate speech, violence, nudity or misinformation
- **Too many reports** from users
- **Suspicious account activity:** New accounts, sudden behavior changes or logins from many locations
- **Automation tools** that break Facebook's rules

In July 2025, Meta said it would cut distribution and monetization for accounts that repeatedly share unoriginal content. It also took action against about **500,000 accounts** for spammy behavior or fake engagement, according to [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/meta-follows-youtube-in-crackdown-on-unoriginal-content/551096/).

## Am I Shadowbanned on Facebook? Signs to Look For

**You may be shadowbanned on Facebook if your reach drops suddenly, your posts don't appear in search or hashtags, and engagement falls with no obvious reason.**

Warning signs:

1. **Sudden reach drop**, often 50% or more, with no change in your posting
2. **Posts don't appear in search results** or hashtag pages
3. **Reels stop getting views** from non-followers
4. **Engagement falls sharply**, even on strong content
5. **Your posts don't show up** for friends or followers who normally see them
6. **Group posts aren't approved** or seem hidden
7. **Warnings in Account Status** or your Professional Dashboard

One bad post isn't a shadowban. Look for a pattern over several days or weeks.

## Is There a Facebook Shadow Ban Checker?

**There's no official Facebook shadow ban checker, but you can check your status using Facebook's own tools and simple tests.**

### 1. Check Account Status

**Go to your Page or professional profile settings and open Account Status to see if any content was removed or marked as not eligible for recommendations.**

### 2. Check Your Professional Dashboard

**Use the Professional Dashboard to review reach, insights and any warnings about distribution or monetization.**

Meta added post-level insights and a tool that lets pages check whether they're at risk of distribution or monetization penalties.

### 3. Search for Your Own Posts

**Log out or ask a friend to search for your page or a recent post to see if it appears.**

### 4. Compare Your Insights

**Compare reach and engagement before and after the drop to spot a sudden change.**

### 5. Test With a Clean Post

**Share a simple, original post and see whether it reaches people normally.**

Be careful with third-party "shadowban checker" websites that ask for your login. Many are unreliable, and some are scams.

## How to Get Unshadowbanned on Facebook

**Find and remove the content that triggered the restriction, stop spammy behavior, pause posting briefly, appeal any violations and return with original, high-quality content.**

### Step 1: Check Account Status for Violations

**Look for removed posts or warnings and appeal any decisions you think are wrong.**

### Step 2: Delete Problem Content

**Remove posts that may break the rules, look like clickbait or reuse other people's content.**

### Step 3: Stop Spammy Behavior

**Stop posting too often, mass tagging, using engagement groups or running automation tools.**

### Step 4: Take a Short Break

**Pause posting for a few days so your account activity looks normal again.**

### Step 5: Post Original Content

**Share content you created yourself, or add real commentary, analysis or storytelling when using others' clips.**

Facebook says remixing is still allowed when creators add meaningful input. It also says views and watch time for original Reels nearly doubled in the second half of 2025 compared with the year before.

### Step 6: Remove Fake Followers and Engagement

**Stop buying likes or followers and remove suspicious accounts if possible.**

### Step 7: Secure Your Account

**Turn on two-factor authentication and remove unknown logins to show your account is trustworthy.**

### Step 8: Contact Meta Support

**If you have access to Meta Verified or business support, ask for help reviewing your account.**

### Step 9: Be Patient

**Recovery can take days or weeks, depending on the cause.**

## How Long Does a Facebook Shadowban Last?

**A Facebook shadowban usually lasts from a few days to a few weeks, but repeat violations can keep reach limited for longer.**

- **Minor issues:** A few days to two weeks
- **Repeated violations:** Several weeks or more
- **Serious violations:** Can lead to monetization loss or account removal

## How to Avoid a Facebook Shadowban

**Post original content, follow Community Standards, avoid engagement bait and spam, and keep your account secure.**

- **Create original content** or add real value to shared clips
- **Avoid engagement bait** and clickbait headlines
- **Don't buy likes or followers**
- **Post at a steady pace**, not in bursts
- **Use trusted links** only
- **Follow Facebook's Community Standards**
- **Check Account Status regularly**

## Shadowban vs Reach Drop: What's the Difference?

**A shadowban means your content is being limited, while a normal reach drop can come from algorithm changes, weaker content or seasonal trends.**

Before assuming a shadowban, check:

- **Algorithm updates** that affect everyone
- **Changes in your content type** or posting times
- **Audience changes**, like fewer active followers
- **Seasonal trends**, like holidays
